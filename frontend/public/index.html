Built differently

Every answer bound
to a verified identity.

Unlike DocuSign or email, Attestr signs at the individual answer level — not the document level. One byte changed anywhere, and you know exactly which answer was tampered with.

⚷
Certificate-based identity
No passwords. X.509 certificates and mTLS mean both sides verify each other before a single byte is exchanged.
🌲
Per-answer Merkle signing
Every answer is individually hashed into a Merkle Tree. ECDSA signs the root. Tamper one answer and the exact failing node glows red.
⟲
Perfect forward secrecy
Ephemeral ECDH keys per session are destroyed after use. Past submissions stay unrecoverable even if keys are stolen in the future.
🪙
The Tessera — self-contained proof
The final sealed bundle contains everything needed to verify it. No platform required. Valid forever.
