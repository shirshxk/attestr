Built differently

Every answer bound
to a verified identity.

Unlike DocuSign or email, Attestr signs at the individual answer level — not the document level. One byte changed anywhere, and you know exactly which answer was tampered with.

⚷
Certificate-based identity
No passwords. X.509 certificates and mTLS mean both sides verify each other before a single byte is exchanged.
🌲
Per-answer Merkle signing
Every answer is individually hashed into a Merkle Tree. ECDSA signs the root. Tamper one answer and the exact failing node glows red.
⟲
Perfect forward secrecy
Ephemeral ECDH keys per session are destroyed after use. Past submissions stay unrecoverable even if keys are stolen in the future.
🪙
The Tessera — self-contained proof
The final sealed bundle contains everything needed to verify it. No platform required. Valid forever.
How it works

From questionnaire to cryptographic proof

Five steps. Every one produces evidence that can be independently verified — no trust in Attestr required.

1
Auditor sends a questionnaire
SOC 2, ISO 27001, or a custom upload. It's assigned to a vendor whose identity is already certified.
2
Vendor answers & signs
Each answer is hashed into a Merkle leaf. The vendor signs the single root with their private key.
3
Bundle is sealed
Answers are encrypted end-to-end and time-stamped. The result is a portable, self-contained Tessera.
4
Auditor verifies
Signature, certificate, timestamp and every answer hash are checked. Tampering shows the exact answer.
5
Proof lasts forever
The Tessera verifies offline, for good. No vendor portal, no expiring link, no platform dependency.
