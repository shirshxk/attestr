Why certificates, not passwords
⚷
Your private key never leaves your device
Authentication proves possession of the private key through a cryptographic challenge. The key itself is never transmitted.
✓
Identity is verified by the CA, not the platform
The Attestr CA issued your certificate. Every mTLS connection verifies it. Even if Attestr's servers are compromised, the certificates remain valid proof of identity.
◈
Cannot be phished
A private key cannot be entered into a fake login form. Phishing attacks that steal passwords are structurally impossible against certificate authentication.
